02About

A little about me.

I am a Graphic and Visual Designer specialising in visual communication, brand identity, packaging design, typography and creative visual storytelling.

My design approach combines thoughtful creative thinking with strong visual execution. I enjoy exploring typography, colour, composition and visual systems to create meaningful and engaging design experiences.

My work spans branding, packaging, social media creatives, digital communication, advertising and print design. I believe good design is not only about aesthetics but also about creating a clear visual identity and communicating the right message.
